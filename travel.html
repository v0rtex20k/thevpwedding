Travel & Lodging

Getting to Andover & Amherst

Airports

For guests traveling to our wedding, there are two convenient airport options.

  • Boston Logan International Airport
    Boston, MA: about 40 minutes from the church, with the most flight options of the two
  • Manchester–Boston Regional Airport
    Manchester, NH: about 15 minutes from our reception venue, a convenient option if you're staying locally for the weekend

Hotel Accommodations

Below are several hotel options conveniently located near either the ceremony or reception venue. We recommend choosing whichever location best fits your plans for the weekend. Feel free to also explore Airbnb, VRBO, and other local lodging in the surrounding area.
