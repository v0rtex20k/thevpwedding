Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

We’d love for you to dress up, but black tie isn’t required. No need for a tux or formal gown.

Is there parking?

There’s plenty of parking at both the church and LaBelle Winery. There won’t be valet service.

How do I RSVP?

We’ll send out RSVP details soon. Check back here for updates.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Space is limited, so your RSVP form will let you know whether a plus-one is included.

Are kids welcome?

Yes, kids are welcome!

Will there be hotel room blocks?

We’ll share room block details once they’re finalized. For now, you can find nearby hotels on our Travel & Lodging page.

Will we be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony is at the church. LaBelle Winery has both indoor and outdoor spaces, including vineyards you can walk through, so bring shoes you’re comfortable walking in.
