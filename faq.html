Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

We’re looking forward to dressing up with you! Formal attire is encouraged, with tuxedos and gowns optional.

Is there parking?

There’s plenty of parking at both the church and LaBelle Winery (~175 spots at each location).

How do I RSVP?

We’ll send out RSVP details soon. Check back here for updates.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Space is limited, so your RSVP form will let you know whether a plus-one is included.

Are kids welcome?

Yes, kids are welcome!

Will we be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be held indoors at the church. LaBelle Winery has both indoor and outdoor spaces, including vineyards and fields you can walk through, so bring shoes you’re comfortable walking around in!
